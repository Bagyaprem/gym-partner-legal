Delete your Gym Partner account

Gym Partner by Bagyaprem

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Gym Partner and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Delete by email (no app needed)

Email support@example.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Gym Partner account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password) and your profile: name, height, weight and all "About you" answers, including health notes. These are permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Workout, food and water logs are stored only on your phone. Deleting your account in the app also erases them; uninstalling the app removes them too.

What is kept

Nothing. We do not keep copies of deleted accounts. Our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a short period (normally no more than 30 days) before they are overwritten.

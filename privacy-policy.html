Gym Partner Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bagyaprem K ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Gym Partner mobile app (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to work:

  • Account information: your email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only in hashed form; we never see your password.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and a link to your Google profile picture with us. We store these with your account to sign you in. We do not receive your Google password or access your Google contacts, Drive or other Google data.
  • Profile information: your name and, only if you choose to answer the optional "About you" questions, your gender, age, height, body weight, fitness goal, training experience, preferred training days per week and gym name. These are stored with your account so they are available when you sign in.
  • Health notes: if you choose to describe injuries or health conditions, that text is stored with your account and used only to show it back to you. This is optional; leave it blank if you prefer not to share it.
  • Fitness and nutrition logs: workouts, sets, weights, reps, cardio sessions, workout plans, meals, food amounts, calories and nutrients, water intake, custom exercises you create, and exercises and foods you save from online search. These are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Meal photos: if you choose to take a photo of your plate or pick one from your gallery, a smaller copy is saved in the App's private storage on your device so it appears in your food diary. Meal photos are never uploaded, shared or analysed, and are deleted when you delete the meal, use "Delete Data on This Device", delete your account, or uninstall the App.

2. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, microphone input, advertising ID, or browsing history, and it does not upload any photos. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Camera: the App asks for camera access only when you tap "Take a photo of your plate". Choosing a photo from your gallery uses the system photo picker, which gives the App only the photo you select. You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone settings.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Create and secure your account, sign you in, and let you reset a forgotten password.
  • Show your name, profile details and "About you" answers inside the App.
  • Send transactional emails you request, such as account confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your account and profile are hosted by Supabase (supabase.com), which provides our authentication and database services and processes data on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments.

If you use Google sign-in, Google (google.com) confirms your identity. Its handling of your Google account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Online search: when you search for exercises or foods, the App sends your search words directly from your phone to public databases: wger (wger.de) for exercises, and USDA FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov) and Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) for foods. Only the search words and standard connection details such as your IP address are sent; your account, profile, workouts and food diary are never sent. These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. Results you open are stored on your device so they work offline.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data storage and security

Account and profile data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and protected by row level security, so each account can access only its own records.

Exercise images in the App are built into the App, so browsing them sends no information to anyone. Searching online sends only your search words, as described in section 4.

App data on your device is excluded from Android cloud backups, so it is not copied to other services.

Logs stored on your device stay on your device. They are removed if you uninstall the App or use "Delete Data on This Device" in Settings. Use "Export My Data" in Settings to keep your own copy.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and profile for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and profile are permanently deleted from our servers, normally immediately and in any case within 30 days.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the App: Profile → Settings → Delete My Account. This permanently deletes your account and profile from our servers and erases App data on your device.
  • In the App: Profile → Settings → Delete Data on This Device erases on-device logs while keeping your account.
  • By email: if you cannot access the App, email gympartner.support@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete your account within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, or to object to its processing. You can do most of this directly in the App. For any other request, or to raise a grievance, contact us at gympartner.support@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

We process your information on the basis of your consent and to provide the service you asked for. Your information may be processed on servers outside your country; we rely on our providers' safeguards when this happens.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Health disclaimer

The App is a fitness logging tool and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition program.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
